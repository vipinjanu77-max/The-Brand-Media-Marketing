## What is reported

Google's AI Overviews put a generated summary above traditional results for many queries. On Alphabet's own figures, the feature reached more than two billion monthly users within about a year of broad launch.

## TBM analysis

*Interpretation by TBM.*

**Visibility is shifting from "ranking" to "being cited".** When an answer is summarised, the question becomes whether your business is mentioned or linked — and whether it is described accurately.

**Answer engines reward clarity and evidence.** In our experience, pages that open with a direct answer, define terms clearly, cite sources and show who wrote them are easier for both people and machines to use.

**Entity consistency matters more.** If your company description, services and locations differ across your website, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and directories, AI systems have conflicting signals to reconcile.

## What to do

1. Identify the 20–50 questions buyers ask before choosing a supplier like you.
2. Answer each directly on the most relevant page, then expand with depth.
3. Publish evidence others can cite — data, methods, examples.
4. Make your entity information consistent everywhere.
5. Track how answer engines describe you, monthly.
