## What the data says

India's advertising market has become digital-first. On the report's estimates, digital went from roughly one rupee in eight of advertising spend in 2016 to nearly three in five by 2025, and it is forecast to approach seven in ten by 2027.

## TBM analysis

*Interpretation by TBM, not a finding of the source report.*

**1. More competition for the same attention.** As budgets move online, auctions on Google, Meta and YouTube get more crowded. Advertisers who win are usually those with better conversion rates and better data feedback, because they can afford to bid more for the same customer.

**2. The split is balanced. So should your thinking be.** No single format dominates. Social, video and search each hold between a fifth and a third of spend. Plans that treat "digital" as a single channel miss the different jobs these formats do: search captures intent, video demonstrates, social creates demand.

**3. Growth in spend is not growth in results.** A 19% rise in spend partly reflects rising media prices. Track your own cost per customer over time rather than assuming market growth will lift your results.

## What to do with this

- Review whether your budget split reflects the jobs you need done (capture vs create demand) rather than habit.
- Invest in conversion-rate and data quality before scaling spend in more competitive auctions.
