## How to use these numbers

National statistics describe the market, not your customers. Use them to **size opportunity and sanity-check assumptions:** for example, whether a social-only strategy can reach your audience, then validate with your own analytics, CRM and customer research.

## TBM analysis

*The following is TBM interpretation, not a finding of the cited sources.*

- **Social alone does not reach most of India's online population.** With fewer than half of internet users on a social platform, businesses that rely only on Instagram or Facebook are likely to miss audiences reachable through search, YouTube and messaging.
- **Digital is now the default budget line, which raises competition.** When most advertising money flows to digital, auctions become more competitive. Efficiency increasingly comes from better conversion, better creative and better data, not simply more spend.
- **Search and video together rival social.** Paid search and online video combined represent roughly half of digital spend in the dentsu-e4m estimates, reinforcing the case for intent (search) and demonstration (video) alongside social.
