## TBM analysis

*Interpretation by TBM.*

**The spread is mostly scope, not margin.** A ₹10,000 SEO plan and a ₹1,00,000 SEO plan are rarely the same service at different prices. The cheaper plan usually covers a few keywords and on-page edits; the expensive one includes technical engineering, content and link earning.

**Percentage-of-spend fees only make sense at scale.** Several guides note (and we agree) that below roughly ₹5 lakh a month of media, a fixed fee better reflects the actual work.

**Where TBM sits.** TBM's ranges sit in the middle-to-upper part of the published agency market, because every engagement includes senior strategy, verified conversion tracking, CRM feedback and outcome-based reporting. We publish what is included and excluded for each tier on the [rate card](/pricing/) so buyers can compare like-for-like.
