## Projections are not guarantees

Marketing outcomes depend on factors including your product, pricing, market, competition, seasonality, platform changes, creative, website, sales process and budget. **No projection, calculator output, benchmark or past result on this website is a guarantee of future performance.**

## Calculators

Our calculators use the assumptions you enter plus clearly labelled planning heuristics. They are designed to help you reason about budgets and targets — not to predict results.

## Case studies

Case studies describe what happened for a specific client, in a specific market, over a specific period. We publish only results verified against platform or CRM data with client approval. Your results may differ.

## Benchmarks

Benchmarks and statistics describe ranges or averages from specific samples. Your business may perform better or worse. We label the source, sample and date of every figure.
