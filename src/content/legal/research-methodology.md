## Our rules

1. **No unsourced numbers.** Every statistic shows its source, publication, year, geography and context — and links to the original.
2. **Label the type of claim.** We distinguish between a **reported figure**, a **third-party estimate**, a **forecast**, **TBM analysis** and **opinion**. A forecast is never presented as a current fact.
3. **Separate TBM data from market data.** Research compiled from other publishers is labelled **Industry Research**. Only research based on data TBM collected itself is labelled **TBM Original Dataset**, with its methodology, sample size, date range and limitations published.
4. **Never generalise small samples.** TBM's own campaign data reflects the accounts we manage. We do not present it as an "India market average".
5. **Verification status is visible.** Newly added statistics show *Pending editorial verification* until an editor has checked the figure against the primary publication; then they show *Editor verified*.
6. **Paraphrase and cite.** We summarise third-party findings in our own words and link to the source rather than reproducing copyrighted material.
7. **Keep it current.** Every research page shows a last-updated date. Superseded figures are updated or clearly marked as historical.

## Source priority

Government and regulator data; platform and company disclosures; established research and industry bodies (for example IAB, WARC, Kantar, Nielsen); consultancies (for example McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte, PwC); and reputable industry publications. Licensed data (for example Statista) is used only where our licence allows.

## Data confidence

TBM datasets carry a visible data-confidence indicator (low / moderate / high) based on sample size, recency, method and representativeness, with the reasoning shown alongside.

Found an error? Tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). We correct errors promptly and note material corrections on the page.
