## The short answer

<div class="answer"><span class="answer-label">Short answer</span>Pick the agency that can show relevant evidence, names the people who will work on your account, builds strategy from your economics, reports on business outcomes, prices transparently, and lets you own your accounts and data.</div>

We built this framework so it can be applied to any agency — including us. Use the [interactive checklist](/tools/agency-evaluation-checklist/) to score agencies side by side and download a report.

## The 14 criteria

| # | Criterion | What good looks like | Question to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relevant experience** | Work in your industry or a similar funnel | "Which businesses like ours have you worked with?" |
| 2 | **Case studies** | Context, baseline, method and outcome | "Walk me through one case study end to end." |
| 3 | **Actual results** | Verifiable numbers, not screenshots without context | "Can we see anonymised platform or CRM evidence?" |
| 4 | **Team** | Named people and their experience | "Who works on our account day to day?" |
| 5 | **Strategy** | Starts from margins, close rates and targets | "How would you set our CPL or CAC target?" |
| 6 | **Reporting** | Pipeline, revenue and CAC — not only clicks | "Can we see a sample report?" |
| 7 | **Tracking** | Measurement plan before spend | "How will you verify conversion tracking?" |
| 8 | **Account ownership** | You own ad accounts, analytics and data | "Whose name are the ad accounts in?" |
| 9 | **Pricing** | Fee, media, production and tools separated | "What is included and excluded?" |
| 10 | **Contract** | Fair notice, clear exit, handover obligations | "What happens if we part ways?" |
| 11 | **Communication** | Defined cadence and escalation | "How often do we meet, and with whom?" |
| 12 | **Industry expertise** | Understands your sales cycle and regulations | "What rules affect marketing in our sector?" |
| 13 | **References** | Current clients you can call | "Can we speak to two current clients?" |
| 14 | **Data security** | Access controls and a data policy | "How do you store and protect our data?" |

## Red flags

- **Guarantees** of rankings, leads or ROAS. Nobody controls platforms, competitors or your sales process.
- **Agency-owned ad accounts.** You lose history and control if you leave.
- **Vanity reporting** — impressions, followers and clicks without leads, customers or revenue.
- **Bundled pricing** that hides how much reaches the media platforms.
- **Senior people in the pitch, juniors on the account** — without disclosure.
- **Unverifiable claims** — awards, certifications or client logos they cannot evidence.

## How to run a fair selection

1. **Write a one-page brief** — objective, budget range, current numbers, constraints. (Download our [campaign brief template](/resources/campaign-brief/).)
2. **Shortlist three agencies** with relevant evidence.
3. **Ask each the same questions** and score them on the 14 criteria.
4. **Request a scoped proposal** with fees, media and production separated.
5. **Call references.**
6. **Agree 90-day success criteria** before signing.
