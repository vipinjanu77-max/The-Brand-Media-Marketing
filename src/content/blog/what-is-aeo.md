## The short answer

<div class="answer"><span class="answer-label">Definition</span>AEO makes your business easy for AI answer engines to understand, quote accurately and cite.</div>

## How does AI search work?

Simplified, most answer engines:

1. **Interpret** the question.
2. **Retrieve** relevant documents from a search index or the web.
3. **Synthesise** an answer with a language model.
4. **Cite** some of the sources used.

Your content has to be retrievable (crawlable, indexed), understandable (clear structure, direct answers) and trustworthy (evidence, authorship, consistency) to be used.

## AEO vs SEO

| | SEO | AEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank and earn clicks | Be cited and described accurately |
| Surface | Search results | AI Overviews, assistants, answer engines |
| Emphasis | Technical, content, links | Direct answers, entities, structured data, evidence |

## AEO checklist

- **Direct answers** in the first lines of key sections
- **Question-led headings** that mirror how buyers ask
- **Clear definitions** of terms you want to be associated with
- **Comparison tables** for common decisions
- **Sourced statistics** with dates and links
- **Author and reviewer information**
- **Consistent entity data** — name, description, services, locations, founders — everywhere
- **Structured data** — Organization, Article, FAQ, Service, Person, Breadcrumb
- **Original evidence** — data, examples and tools others will reference

## What AEO cannot do

No one can guarantee inclusion in AI answers. AEO improves the conditions for accurate citation; it does not control the outcome. Be wary of fake mentions, fake reviews or manipulative tactics — they create long-term risk.
