## The short answer

<div class="answer"><span class="answer-label">Short answer</span>Expect 4-12 months for meaningful commercial SEO results, with faster wins from technical fixes and lower-competition pages.</div>

## A realistic timeline

| Phase | What happens | What to track |
|---|---|---|
| **Months 1-2** | Audit, technical fixes, strategy, priority pages optimised | Indexation, crawl errors, Core Web Vitals |
| **Months 3-4** | New and improved pages indexed; early movement on lower-competition terms | Impressions in Search Console, average position |
| **Months 5-6** | Clicks rise on long-tail and mid-competition terms | Non-brand clicks, organic leads |
| **Months 6-12** | Authority compounds; competitive terms move | Organic conversions and revenue |

## What makes SEO faster

- An established domain with some existing authority
- Severe technical problems whose fix unlocks existing content
- Low-competition niches or local markets
- Content that is genuinely better than what ranks

## What makes it slower

- New domains, highly competitive categories, YMYL topics (health, finance)
- Thin or generic content
- Slow implementation of recommendations
