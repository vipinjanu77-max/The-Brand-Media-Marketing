## The short answer

<div class="answer"><span class="answer-label">Short answer</span>There is no single price for digital marketing in India. Cost depends on which channels you use, how competitive your market is, the scale you want and how much work is included. Always separate four components: <strong>agency fee</strong>, <strong>media spend</strong>, <strong>production</strong> and <strong>technology</strong>.</div>

## The four components of cost

**Agency fee ≠ media spend ≠ production cost ≠ technology cost.**

| Component | What it pays for | Who receives it | Scales with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agency / team fee** | Strategy, management, optimisation, reporting, expertise | Agency, freelancer or in-house team | Scope, complexity, seniority |
| **Media spend** | Ad impressions and clicks | Google, Meta, LinkedIn, publishers | Your growth ambition and CPC/CPM |
| **Production** | Creative, video, photography, content, landing pages | Agency, studio or creators | Volume and quality of assets |
| **Technology** | CRM, automation, analytics, WhatsApp platform, hosting, tools | Software vendors | Users, contacts, messages |

When a quote gives one number, ask how it breaks down. A ₹50,000 "package" that includes ₹30,000 of media leaves ₹20,000 for management and creative — which buys very different work from a ₹50,000 management fee plus a separate media budget.

## Cost by service

We don't publish invented "market average" prices. Below is what drives cost up or down for each service. ### SEO
- **Drivers:** number of pages, technical condition, competition for your keywords, content volume, link-earning effort, local vs national vs international.
- **Cheaper when:** small site, local market, you write content in-house.
- **More expensive when:** large e-commerce catalogue, migration, competitive national terms, international targeting.

### Google Ads management
- **Common fee models:** fixed monthly fee, percentage of spend, or hybrid with a minimum.
- **Separate media budget** paid to Google. Set it from your target CPL/CPA and the conversions needed for bidding algorithms to learn.
- **Setup** (tracking, account build) is often a one-time charge.

### Meta Ads management
- **Three separate lines:** management, creative production, media spend.
- Creative volume is the hidden cost — performance on Meta depends on continuously testing new creative.

### Social media management
- **Drivers:** number of platforms, posts per month, share of video, design complexity, community management hours.

### Website development
- **Drivers:** number of unique templates, content migration, integrations (CRM, payments, booking), CMS requirements, performance and accessibility standards.
- Landing pages are priced per page; corporate and e-commerce sites per project; custom builds are scoped.

### Content marketing
- **Drivers:** depth of research, subject-matter expert input, original data, editing and review, distribution.

### Branding
- **Drivers:** research depth, number of stakeholders, naming, identity system scope, guidelines and templates.

### Performance marketing (multi-channel)
- Management fees scale with the number of channels and the complexity of tracking and reporting, plus media across platforms.

### Influencer marketing
- **Drivers:** creator reach and category, deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity, and whether paid amplification is included. Disclosure compliance is non-negotiable.

### Video marketing
- **Drivers:** scripting, talent, locations, crew, editing, animation and number of cut-downs.

### CRM and marketing automation
- **Technology:** CRM and WhatsApp/email platform subscriptions, usually priced by users, contacts or messages.
- **Setup:** pipeline design, integrations, lead scoring, workflows and dashboards.

## Starter, Growth, Scale, Enterprise

Complexity varies too much for fixed prices to be honest across every business. These tiers describe *scope*, not a price:

| Tier | Typical situation | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Early-stage or local business, one or two channels | Foundations: tracking, one paid channel, local SEO, basic content |
| **Growth** | Proven offer, ready to expand | Two to four channels, CRO, content programme, monthly strategy |
| **Scale** | Multiple channels, larger budgets | Full-funnel programme, creative engine, automation, advanced analytics |
| **Enterprise** | Multiple brands, markets or business units | Dedicated team, governance, custom reporting, integrations |

## How to set your budget

1. **Start from economics.** What gross profit does a customer produce? What CAC can you afford?
2. **Work backwards to media.** Required customers ÷ conversion rates = required leads; × realistic CPL = media budget.
3. **Add the people and production needed** to run that media well.
4. **Add technology** for tracking, CRM and automation.
5. **Keep a contingency** for testing.

Our [digital marketing cost calculator](/tools/digital-marketing-cost-calculator/) gives a starting range split into these components, and the [ROI calculator](/tools/marketing-roi-calculator/) works backwards from a revenue target.

## Red flags in pricing

- One number with no breakdown between fee, media and production
- Ad accounts owned by the agency, not you
- Guaranteed rankings, leads or ROAS
- Long lock-ins without performance review points
- Reporting limited to impressions, clicks and followers
